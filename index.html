Trust is part of the product

Transparent by design. Human in control.

ProfitPilot's foundations are being shaped around clear evidence, fair treatment and accountable decisions.

◎

Traceable numbers

Preserve source records, calculation methods and review history so results can be understood and checked.

◇

Fair outcomes

Make room for review, correction and proportionate resolution. A claim is not automatically a finding.

⌑

Human authority

AI may assist with extraction and explanation, but must not independently approve payments, disputes or ratings.
