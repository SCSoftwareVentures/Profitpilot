# Validation Gates — G0 to G6

These gates control progression from discovery to concierge validation, paid demand, software build and post-build pilots. A roadmap item does not override a failed gate.

## G0 — Data feasibility and competitive proof
Obtain at least **five complete, authorised export sets from at least three unrelated businesses**.

At least 80% of all unique complete sets must meet each per-set test:
- at least 80% of the payroll pool attributable using direct coding or an explicitly approved allocation method;
- at least 80% of eligible invoice value matched with medium- or high-confidence evidence;
- one-site baseline creation at or below 120 customer minutes and 180 analyst minutes;
- source row counts and monetary totals reconcile, with tax basis and rounding treatment documented.

Complete hands-on teardown of at least five relevant products and two actual customer tool stacks using the same scenario pack. At least two independent target customers must demonstrate a material recurring gap not adequately solved by current tools/configuration; at least one buyer must confirm willingness to pay.

Use only data with documented authority and an appropriate privacy basis. Synthetic or de-identified data may support workflow testing but does not establish production data feasibility. Stop or pivot if there is no lawful, repeatable data path.

## G1 — Problem validation
- 20 interviews across at least three recruitment channels.
- Founder-network share no greater than 40%.
- At least 12 of 20 independently describe the same material pain before the product is explained.
- At least eight quantified recent examples, including current workaround and identifiable buyer.

## G2 — Concierge workflow feasibility
- Three unpaid/no-obligation concierge trials across at least three businesses.
- Demonstrate repeatable source-to-variance-bridge workflow.
- At least two of three users confirm that the output informs or changes a real decision.

## G3 — Paid demand
- Ten qualified buying conversations.
- At least three paid concierge engagements or signed paid commitments.
- If this threshold is not met, do not proceed to software build.

## G4 — Prototype usability
- Eight moderated usability sessions.
- At least six of eight participants complete core tasks without facilitator rescue.
- Record numeric first-insight time for all eight; median target is 30 minutes or less.

## G5 — Formal software-build authorisation
Software build is prohibited until G0–G4 and competitive preconditions pass. G5 requires an explicit signed GO with a named technical owner, legal and privacy plan, demonstrated delivery capacity, and approved budget and resourcing. A roadmap, prototype or technical enthusiasm is not build authorisation.

## G6 — Post-build paid software-pilot exit
After an authorised software build:
- three paid software pilots;
- at least two of three use the product weekly for four consecutive weeks;
- at least 30% reduction in an agreed reconciliation-effort measure, or another outcome agreed in advance;
- zero unresolved critical defects;
- credible support and gross-margin model.

G6 is a post-build exit gate, not a build-entry condition.

## Gate reporting rules
For every gate, record owner and review date, evidence source and permission basis, numerator and denominator, exclusions, pass/fail/blocked status, unresolved exceptions, reviewer and decision, and method version. Missing evidence is not a pass. Incomplete periods remain separately identified. Historical test results must not be represented as freshly rerun.
