# Strict Australian legal and privacy release standard
Policy version AU-STRICT-1; 18 November 2026. First-party engineering policy, not legal advice, counsel approval or certification. Proposed stricter product rules are not claims that Australian law necessarily requires them. Scope: Australian business operations and management decision support; future modules require reassessment. Production stays closed.

## Mandatory baseline and release evidence

Every row requires a written counsel disposition for the actual entity, current law, feature scope and deployment configuration; an opaque private evidence digest; technical/operational evidence where relevant; and separate owner acceptance. No conditional pass or waiver of mandatory gates. If a law genuinely does not apply, counsel must document reasoning and accepted alternative safeguards in the evidence before signing approval. Review all APPs, including APP 2 anonymity where practicable, APP 4 unsolicited records, APP 7 marketing and APP 9 government identifiers.

| ID | Required closure evidence |
|---|---|
| AU01 | Legal entity and applicability: Privacy Act, jurisdiction/turnover/exceptions, customer/provider responsibilities, applicable state/sector requirements, current legal obligations register. Apply APP-equivalent baseline voluntarily even if exempt. |
| AU02 | Collection and purpose: approved field-level data map, lawful and fair collection, privacy notice at collection, permitted uses, unsolicited data handling, legitimate purpose and minimisation; prohibit sensitive health data, TFNs, payment-card data and bank account details unless a separately approved feature requires them. Never describe detection as infallible. |
| AU03 | Security: approved data classification, encryption and key governance, tenant/role isolation, MFA, supplier access, security testing, independent penetration testing and measured recovery. Technical controls plus organisational procedures and named accountability. |
| AU04 | Retention: record-specific legal purpose, period and authority, legal holds, correction/supersession, controlled destruction or de-identification, backup and subprocessors, evidence of deletion and restore fencing. Confirmed payroll evidence currently has an immutability freeze: no live payroll until lawful correction and disposal are engineered and independently reviewed. No blanket indefinite or seven-year rule. |
| AU05 | Rights: verified access/correction, authorised representative handling, appropriate response and refusal reasons, redaction, requests and complaint escalation; no charging for making access or correction requests or correcting information; confirm any permissible cost-based access fee and prohibit it by product default. Internal target acknowledge within 2 business days, resolve within 20 calendar days where practicable, escalation before day 30; law requires reasonable period for organisations, not universal 20/30-day deadline. |
| AU06 | Breach response: identify grounds for suspected eligible breach, containment and documented assessment of serious harm/remediation, reasonable steps to complete assessment within 30 calendar days; eligible breach notification as soon as practicable where required. Internal escalation within 1 hour and targeting triage within 24 hours; complete exercise with counsel and actual contacts. No automated notices. |
| AU07 | Overseas disclosure and vendors: initially propose Australia-region hosting, backups and approved support; verify actual storage, remote access, subprocessors and jurisdiction. No transfer to unapproved recipient; counsel evaluates APP 8/S16C circumstances, safeguards and disclosures. No unverified Australia-only guarantee. |
| AU08 | Automated decisions: catalogue each algorithm, personal information categories and decisions significantly affecting individual rights/interests. Review APP 1.7–1.9 from 10 December 2026 where applicable. No solely automated consequential employment, customer eligibility or dispute decisions by product policy; meaningful human review, reasons, correction and contestability; human review does not automatically avoid disclosure obligations. |
| AU09 | Employment/payroll/tax: map Fair Work employer time and wage records (generally seven years), verify needed record subsets and correction history; avoid employee-record exemption assumption for a contractor, no TFN collection without separately verified lawful authority. Distinguish management allocation from statutory payroll, STP, tax advice or wage calculation; assess tax record and state laws with counsel. |
| AU10 | Contracts/consumer law: counsel-reviewed customer terms, privacy policy, collection notice, processing agreement and exit/deletion agreement; transparent prices, renewal, cancellation and remedies; no unfair standard-form terms, misleading marketing, exclusions of non-excludable rights or unconditional security guarantees. |
| AU11 | Regulated perimeter: keep real payment custody, escrow, staged release, financial product advice and regulated services disabled unless specialist counsel confirms licensing, applicable financial services and AML/CTF obligations, provider arrangements and separate explicit release approval. No initial funds holding merely via a disclaimer. |
| AU12 | Operational governance: approved privacy officer/contact, rights and breach runbooks, current vendor inventory, staff training, audit trail, independent reports, privacy impact assessment, change/review process and verified actual notices. No real data before all applicable requirements are satisfied. |

## Review triggers and exact binding
Repeat assessment before changes to code, feature scope, settings, hosting, subprocessors, retention, law or handling purposes. Legal and owner sign-off must bind to exact release commit and independently approved scope/configuration SHA-256. Gate approvals expire within 90 days (internal policy; reapprove earlier when change occurs), and every item must be approved rather than merely risk-accepted. Keep private evidence under controlled custody. Public repo contains only the standard and synthetic test fixtures; no real client/employee records, legal advice, private keys, reviewer identities or privileged reports.

## Status
No gate has external approval. The offline checker verifies signed document shape, exact release binding, freshness and all required approvals; it cannot validate a lawyer's qualification, evidence truth, key custody, real configuration or legal compliance. It is not itself a deployed enforcement boundary; make it mandatory in the independently controlled production release pipeline before any live use. No production activation, contact, payment or publication authorised.

## Offline release evidence verification

The checker is `scripts/verify-australian-compliance.js`. A protected release operator supplies a private bundle and independently established release commit, scope/configuration digest, distinct counsel and owner public keys and their pinned SHA-256 fingerprints (SPKI DER). Each signer signs the byte-for-byte canonical statement from `canonicalApproval(record)` using its separately controlled Ed25519 private key. The record contains policy, commit, scopeSha256, approvedAt, expiresAt and ordered AU01–AU12 gates; each gate must be approved and bind a nonzero SHA-256 digest of its private evidence. Bundle fields are record, counselSignature and ownerSignature (canonical base64). Store the approval bundle outside the public repository.

```sh
node scripts/verify-australian-compliance.js PRIVATE_BUNDLE EXACT_COMMIT SCOPE_SHA256 COUNSEL_PUBLIC_KEY OWNER_PUBLIC_KEY COUNSEL_FINGERPRINT OWNER_FINGERPRINT
```

Nonzero exit denies approval. The verifier does not currently run as a production deployment gate because no production pipeline or independently pinned trust roots exist. Integrate under an independently controlled protected release workflow with no bypass before production. Never use generated synthetic signers or a CI pass as authority. On legal changes, configuration changes or compromised keys, revoke the current release approval externally and run the gate against the new independent trust configuration. No genuine approval bundle has been created.
