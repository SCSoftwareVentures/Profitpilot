# ProfitPilot — Product Overview

## Product definition
ProfitPilot is a proposed business operations and customer transaction-management platform. Its core promise is to help businesses understand and manage financial performance and customer transactions through traceable records, transparent decisions and fair outcomes.

The overall vision is broader than the first release. The entry point remains an evidence-led contract economics workflow for recurring commercial cleaning.

## Initial customer hypothesis
Australian independent commercial cleaning contractors with recurring, multi-site business-to-business contracts. This is a target-customer hypothesis, not a validated market finding.

## Initial problem hypothesis
A contractor may struggle to connect agreed contract scope and price, labour and other attributable delivery costs, site-level operational records, approved variations, and invoice outcomes.

The product should expose gaps, mismatches and uncertainty rather than manufacture a precise-looking profit figure from incomplete data.

## Primary unit
**Contract × site × service period.** Visit-level metrics are excluded unless reliable source attendance or quality evidence is available and in scope.

## Financial truth
- Payroll is authoritative for pay-period payroll cost.
- Site allocation is a management allocation unless site-coded timesheets or equivalent source evidence support direct attribution.
- Unallocated and non-site costs remain visible; they must not be silently forced into a site.
- Allocation methods, assumptions, source periods and reconciliation differences must be traceable.

## Variations and invoice matching
- Detect possible scope or price variations as candidates.
- Preserve source evidence and review status.
- Do not infer contractual approval from an operational note or detected change.
- Do not classify a candidate variation as billable revenue without authorised approval evidence.
- Invoice matching produces confidence-scored suggestions requiring human confirmation.
- Preserve pending, accepted, rejected, held/unmatched and duplicate-candidate states.

## Concierge-first delivery
The first delivery mode is a concierge validation service using secure, authorised data and manual or scripted reconciliation. Automate only repeated steps after evidence demonstrates usefulness and repeatability. Start with one representative site and progressively enrich the analysis.

## Expanded platform vision
Later areas may include quote and scope acceptance, customer–business job records, milestone workflows, completion evidence, fair issue resolution, business profiles, transaction-grounded reputation, aftercare records and payment-provider orchestration.

These are future directions, not committed first-release scope. Payment custody, safeguarding, escrow-like arrangements, dispute adjudication and regulated financial activities require separate legal and provider feasibility work before implementation or marketing claims.

## Product integrity
A photo or completion declaration is evidence, not automatic proof of satisfactory work. A complaint is a claim, not automatic proof of a defect. Consequential decisions must be explainable, reviewable and proportionate. Customer statutory rights must not be displaced by platform terms.

## Current status
The V8.6.1 specification is an audited working product and validation specification. It is not customer-validated, legally cleared or production-ready. The product thesis remains a hypothesis until the defined evidence gates pass.
