# Workspace and commercial baseline candidate
Migration 066 creates managed business, customer organization alias, site, contract and explicit contract-site records with stable scoped UUIDs, recent MFA, owner/admin role, audit history and an immutable private catalog. Existing public business/site/contract tables are populated without blanket write access. Customer names are bounded aliases; screening is conservative, not proof of de-identification. Managed contracts require explicit same-business site assignment for confirmed invoice match and calculations. Once the first baseline is recorded, assignments freeze; further site/scope amendments require a future versioned amendment workflow. Legacy synthetic fixtures remain compatible. Baselines use AUD, finite nonoverlapping effective periods, exact decimal price, known tax/billing basis and evidence digest; distinct requester and approver and single-use consumption. The scope evidence is a reviewed pointer; full service inclusions/exclusions, operational scope and tax/financial validity remain unverified, and scopeIncomplete is explicit. No edit/delete or archive lifecycle, tenant provisioning, non-AUD policy, customer portal, real source docs or live processing. The candidate UI and API support synthetic setup and independent approvals; real browser usability remains unverified. Recovery inventory includes the three ledgers and must be regenerated. No production activation.
