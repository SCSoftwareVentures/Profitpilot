# Isolated privacy transport acceptance exercise

The test-only courier in tests/support uses actual TLS 1.3, mutual certificate authentication, an independently supplied exact server certificate pin, a separately pinned recipient TLS certificate, and a random 256-bit scoped bearer token. It can bind only to an ephemeral port on 127.0.0.1. No configurable address or destination, redirects, proxy, DNS lookup, live external network, persistent queue, documents, or production route are introduced. TLS fixtures are generated ephemerally with OpenSSL. All records, keys and credentials are synthetic.

The offer binds an existing exact sender-signed challenge and approved envelope digest. Claim is atomically consumed before transmission, expired offers refuse claim/acknowledgement, and the recipient signs its acknowledgement only after authenticating and decrypting the exact envelope. The courier verifies the signed acknowledgement and atomically records one result in memory. It cannot claim actual delivery, identity or reading; deliveryVerified is always false. A lost claim is not automatically retried. The state is deliberately non-durable and does not authorize real release after process restart.

The actual authenticated PostgreSQL/API integration test now generates the envelope and cryptographic key pairs, records the two-person approved release, performs the loopback transfer, verifies the signed acknowledgement, uses its exact digests for the separate two-person receipt review and confirms the persisted database evidence matches. CI also runs adversarial local TLS exercises for wrong CA, peer pin, client certificate, bearer, expired challenge, concurrent claim/receipt, altered envelope/signature, wrong recipient and cross-release receipt. The test harness is not a production transport implementation. Independent operational and legal acceptance, real recipient identity/key custody, revocation, durable one-time dispatch and recovery reconciliation, approved external channel and actual live delivery remain outstanding. No independent certification or live disclosure is claimed.
