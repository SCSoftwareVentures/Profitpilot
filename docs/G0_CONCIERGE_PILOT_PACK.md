# G0 Concierge Pilot Pack

This pack operationalises the V8.6.1 G0 data-feasibility test. It is a planning and evidence template, not permission to collect or process data. Obtain documented authority and approve the handling route before any transfer.

## 1. G0 objective

Determine whether ProfitPilot can repeatedly create a traceable one-site contract economics variance bridge from lawful, sufficiently complete source records at an effort level that can support a viable service.

G0 requires at least five complete, authorised export sets from at least three unrelated businesses.

At least 80% of all unique complete sets must meet every per-set test:
- at least 80% of the payroll pool attributable using direct coding or an explicitly approved allocation basis;
- at least 80% of eligible invoice value matched with medium- or high-confidence evidence;
- one-site baseline completed within 120 customer minutes and 180 analyst minutes;
- source row counts and monetary totals reconcile under a documented tax and rounding basis.

A missing or unauthorised source is not a pass. If there is no lawful data route or repeatable source path, stop. If a specific source blocks the workflow, record a pivot decision.

## 2. Minimum source bundle

Request only the fields needed for the agreed one-site and service-period test. The exact field list must be approved with the business before collection.

| Source component | Minimum purpose | Required control |
|---|---|---|
| Contract, site and scope/pricing | Establish approved commercial baseline | Record contract/scope version, site identity, service period and source reference |
| Payroll or timesheet export | Establish pay-period payroll pool and available labour coding | Payroll total remains authoritative; identify whether hours/costs are direct, allocated or unallocated |
| Supplier/subcontractor costs | Capture relevant non-payroll delivery cost | Preserve invoice/source reference, period, site attribution and GST basis where supplied |
| Customer invoice lines | Compare eligible invoice value with contract/site/service period | Match as candidate with evidence and confidence; human review required |

Do not request full customer databases, bank credentials, passwords, unrelated employee information or broad accounting exports where a narrower extract will suffice.

## 3. Permission and handling record

Complete before any data transfer:
- business legal name and authorised contact;
- identity/authority of person approving the export;
- purpose and scope of the pilot;
- source systems and export periods;
- data fields and categories included;
- whether personal or employee-identifiable information is present;
- permission/notice basis confirmed by the business;
- approved transfer channel and access restrictions;
- authorised recipients and any subprocessors;
- storage location, retention period and deletion/return date;
- incident contact and escalation route;
- pilot agreement and any required data-processing terms.

If these controls cannot be established, do not accept the dataset. Synthetic or appropriately de-identified data may be used for workflow rehearsal, but does not count as a real G0 export set.

## 4. Export-set register

Create one record per unique business × source period × bundle. Record:
- export-set ID;
- business pseudonym;
- source period;
- bundle components present;
- authorisation status and evidence reference;
- row counts and source totals by component;
- duplicate-set check;
- completeness decision and reviewer;
- exclusions and reason.

Do not count duplicate exports or multiple copies of the same underlying period as independent complete sets.

## 5. One-site variance bridge

For one representative site and service period, produce:

### A. Commercial baseline
- contract price and billing method;
- approved scope summary and service frequency;
- planned hours or estimated labour basis;
- known consumables and subcontractor costs;
- source/version references;
- assumptions and confidence.

### B. Cost bridge
- payroll/timesheet source and pay period;
- total payroll pool;
- direct-coded amount;
- approved allocated amount and method;
- non-site amount;
- unallocated residual;
- supplier/subcontractor cost;
- missing cost categories and exceptions.

Payroll remains authoritative for pay-period payroll cost. Site cost is a management allocation unless supported by site-coded source records. Never silently force unallocated or non-site amounts into a site.

### C. Invoice bridge
- eligible invoice lines;
- candidate site/contract/service-period match;
- evidence supporting the match;
- confidence level;
- unmatched or held items;
- reviewer decision and date.

### D. Variation register
- requested, observed, approved or rejected status;
- source evidence;
- estimated cost/price impact;
- approval authority;
- invoice/billing outcome.

A detected change is a candidate exception, not an approved or billable variation.

### E. Action summary
- exception and source;
- quantified amount or range, if supportable;
- confidence and limitation;
- owner and due date;
- customer decision;
- confirmed outcome.

## 6. Effort and quality measures

Record actual minutes, not retrospective guesses:
- customer preparation minutes;
- customer review minutes;
- analyst handling minutes;
- elapsed time and waiting time separately;
- mapping and exception count;
- percentage of payroll pool attributable under the approved basis;
- percentage of eligible invoice value matched at medium/high evidence;
- source row and monetary reconciliation differences;
- number and value of unresolved exceptions;
- whether the output changed or informed a real decision.

Do not count elapsed waiting time as analyst handling time. Record rework separately.

## 7. G0 result calculation

Denominator: all unique complete, authorised export sets. Keep incomplete and unauthorised bundles visible in the register but do not silently include them as complete sets or treat them as passes.

For each complete set, record PASS only when all four per-set criteria pass. G0 passes the export-set threshold only when at least 80% of the complete sets pass and the minimum of five sets across three unrelated businesses is met.

A complete-set pass does not by itself satisfy the separate competitive-proof requirement. Record the hands-on competitor teardown and actual customer-stack evidence independently.

## 8. Competitive proof

Use the same anonymised scenario pack and success criteria for:
- at least five relevant product teardowns;
- at least two actual customer tool stacks.

Record product/stack, plan and add-ons, configuration effort, operator skill, data required, matching accuracy, exception handling, traceability, time-on-task, total cost and customer trust.

Public marketing pages are discovery evidence only. Do not claim a competitor lacks a capability without hands-on testing or authoritative documentation.

Competitive precondition: at least two independent target customers demonstrate a material recurring gap not adequately solved by current tools/configuration, and at least one buyer confirms willingness to pay.

## 9. Stop / pivot rules

STOP if there is no lawful data route or no repeatable source path.

PIVOT if a specific source, attribution method, customer segment or current-system limitation blocks repeatability but a narrower lawful path appears testable.

Do not progress to software build based on enthusiasm, synthetic data, a single friendly business, incomplete periods or unverified assumptions.

## 10. Evidence record

For every decision retain:
- evidence ID and source reference;
- permission basis;
- date and reviewer;
- method/version;
- denominator and exclusions;
- calculation or reconciliation;
- result and unresolved limitations;
- customer confirmation where relevant.

This pack does not replace legal/privacy review, a pilot agreement or specialist advice.
