# Initial Risk Register

| Risk | Why it matters | Required control / evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Weak site-level labour attribution | Misleading contract margin or variance | Reconcile payroll pool; separate direct, allocated and unallocated amounts; disclose method |
| Excessive baseline effort | First value may be uneconomic | Measure customer and analyst minutes during G0 |
| Informal variation capture | Unapproved changes may be misclassified as revenue | Candidate-only status; preserve source; require approval evidence |
| Invoice/site matching errors | Wrong contract economics | Confidence-scored suggestions; human confirmation; exception queue |
| Subcontractor cost gaps | Incomplete delivery economics | Define source requirements and attribution treatment before claims |
| Scope expansion | Delays validation and increases cost | Preserve beachhead, gates, non-goals and signed scope decisions |
| Employee privacy | Sensitive workforce data and trust impacts | Data minimisation, pseudonymisation, small-cell suppression and legal review |
| Payment custody / safeguarding | Regulatory, insolvency and customer-funds exposure | Authorised provider and specialist legal/provider sign-off before design commitment |
| Dispute bias or unfairness | Customer/business trust and legal exposure | Clear job acceptance schedule, proportional remedies, evidence access, human review and appeal |
| Fraudulent or manipulated evidence | Incorrect payment/reputation outcomes | Provenance, metadata controls, review process and escalation |
| Review manipulation / retaliation | Reputational harm and platform liability | Transaction-grounded reviews, moderation, response and appeal controls |
| Security breach | Customer, employee and commercial harm | Threat model, least privilege, tenant isolation, security testing and incident plan |
| Unproven willingness to pay | Product may not have a viable buyer | G1–G3 evidence and paid concierge threshold |
| Unviable unit economics | Support, payment and dispute costs may exceed revenue | Model acquisition, support, payment, fraud, assessment and compliance costs |
| Provider or platform failure | Funds, records or operations may be disrupted | Reconciliation, continuity, exit, portability and escalation plans |

This is an initial specification-level register, not a completed risk assessment. Owners, likelihood, impact, residual risk and review dates must be added during an authorised delivery phase.
