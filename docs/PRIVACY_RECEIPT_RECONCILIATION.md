# Privacy signed receipt and reconciliation (candidate only)

The offline privacy-delivery-receipt.js helper binds a sender-signed random challenge to the exact authorized release, tenant, case, order, scope, response and envelope digest, and independently pinned X25519 encryption key and distinct Ed25519 recipient receipt-signing key. Expected metadata and key fingerprints MUST come from independently authenticated authority, never from a submitted challenge. The sender challenge can be issued only within 15 minutes of authorization, expires after 15 minutes, and the supplied recipient helper signs only after authenticating and decrypting the approved envelope. Offline verification checks exact bindings, both Ed25519 signatures, bounded timestamps, freshness and canonical encoding; reconciliation accepts signed statements no more than 24 hours after acknowledgement. No plaintext or key material is included in the receipt. A key signature is evidence of a statement, not proof of physical receipt, identity, legal entitlement, or reading. The signer could sign without using the helper; timestamps are claimed, not trusted external timestamps. A verified signature never returns deliveryVerified=true.

Migration 058 records digests of externally verified receipt, challenge, independently verified receipt-signing key and verification evidence only after a separate dual-approved, fresh-MFA work order. One immutable record per release, unique receipt/challenge per tenant, exact case and release scope binding, replay rejection and signed recovery fingerprint. This does not cryptographically verify the receipt inside SQL or authenticate external evidence; it records an independently reviewed assertion. The existing operator status distinguishes receiptEvidenceRecorded from deliveryVerified (always false). No new runtime grants or transport, no live deployment. Actual external key custody, recipient verification, transport, receipt processing, revocation/recovery, legal review and independent operational acceptance remain required. All tests and database fixtures use synthetic data. Never publish real receipt bundles, keys, documents or digests in this public repo.
